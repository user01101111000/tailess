# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help.

## What that means here

- **Critique the code, not the person.** "This misses the case where the value is a
  template" is useful. "This is sloppy" is not.
- **Say why.** A change request with a reason is something a contributor can act on; one
  without is something they have to guess at.
- **Take the time zone.** Reviews and answers may take days. Nobody here is on call.
- **A question is not an imposition.** Most of them are documentation that has not been
  written yet.

## Not welcome

Harassment, insults, or personal attacks; unwelcome attention of any kind; publishing
anyone's private information; and sustained disruption of a discussion. This applies in
issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages alike.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/user01101111000). Reports are read, kept in confidence, and
answered.

Depending on what happened, the response ranges from a private word to editing or
removing the content to blocking the account. The maintainer decides, and will say what
was decided to the person who reported it.
